Goats

A deterministic simulation for projectiles on 2d terrain, that includes: terrain as a bitmap mask, a fixed-timestep integrator, collision detection by sampling, surface normal estimation, and reflection with energy loss. 

Basic structure:

Player:
id
name
location
health

Level:
collision map
terrain color
background color

World:
list of players
current level

State:
active player
mode
simulation step
