goats

a small, deterministic simulation for projectiles on 2d terrain, and that includes: terrain as a bitmap mask, a fixed-timestep integrator, collision detection by sampling, surface normal estimation, and reflection with energy loss. 

basic structure and requirements:

player:
id
name
location
health

level:
collision map
terrain color
background color

world:
list of players
current level

state:
active player
mode
simulation step
